### [Minion Killfeed](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1152)
<img width="502" height="416" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/57653a18-1124-40f8-9b5e-180819cfc98f" />

Standalone mod that broadcasts minion kills (such as Groaners and Poxwalkers) directly in the combat kill feed. It intercepts trash mob deaths and clusters identical kills into clean stacks (e.g., Poxwalker x5) to prevent feed spam, while automatically stepping aside to let Killfeed Improvements handle elites and Companion Killfeed handle pets. You can configure it to filter out minion kills made by your teammates and toggle the stacking feature on or off.
