Adds horde and trash mob kills (Poxwalkers, Groaners) to the killfeed, stacked (e.g., Poxwalker x5) to prevent feed spam.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]General[/b]
- Enable Minion Kills
- Stack Minion Kills
- Show Teammate Kills
- Show Own Kills
- Show Enemy-on-Enemy Kills
